# Privacy and Legal Assumptions

Related issue: #17  
Source: HEDS-029 Legal / Terms / Privacy / SLA Package

## Assumptions

- Tenant/customer data must remain scoped to authorized users.
- Forecast and plant data may be commercially sensitive.
- User identity and audit records may be personal data depending on jurisdiction.
- Notifications must avoid exposing sensitive details.
- Logs must avoid secrets, raw credentials, and unnecessary personal data.

## Required future work

- Confirm privacy policy obligations.
- Confirm data retention periods.
- Confirm SLA commitments.
- Confirm data export/delete workflows where required.
- Confirm subprocessors and provider terms.
